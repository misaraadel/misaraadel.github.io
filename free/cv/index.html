البرومبت

Here is my CV and the job description I'm applying for.
Rewrite my CV so ATS (applicant tracking systems) can read it correctly:

- One column only. No tables, no text boxes, no images, no icons, no skill bars, no header/footer content.
- Standard section headings: Summary, Experience, Projects, Skills, Education.
- Use the exact keywords and skills from the job description ONLY where they truthfully match my experience.
- Do NOT invent any experience, company, title, number, or skill I don't have.
  If something important from the job description is missing, list it at the end under "Gaps to consider" instead of adding it.
- Bullet points that start with action verbs and show results.
- Keep it to one page with a simple, standard font.

Give me: 1) the final CV text, ready to paste into Word or Google Docs, and 2) the list of keywords you matched.

My CV: [paste or attach]
Job description: [paste]

نصايح

  • احفظه PDF أو DOCX عادي من Word أو Google Docs، مش صورة ولا تصميم Canva مليان أشكال.
  • اعمل نسخة لكل وظيفة، لأن كل إعلان ليه كلمات مختلفة.
  • ما تكتبش حاجة مش حقيقية. الانترفيو هيكشفها.
